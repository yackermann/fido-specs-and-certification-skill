# FIDO Certification Request Agreement

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/secretariat/certification-request-agreement/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read certification-request-agreement`)

## Summary

A short privacy click-through that appears before the certification request form. The requesting company accepts that FIDO processes its staff's contact data under the FIDO privacy policy, with transfer to the US. The company must tell its staff and get any consent the law requires. EU/EEA individuals can raise GDPR requests with FIDO's Data Protection Officer.
