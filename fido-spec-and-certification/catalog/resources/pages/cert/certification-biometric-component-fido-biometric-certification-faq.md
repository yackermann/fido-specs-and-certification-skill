# FIDO Biometric Certification FAQ

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification-biometric-component-fido-biometric-certification-faq/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read certification-biometric-component-fido-biometric-certification-faq`)

## Summary

FAQ covering FIDO's two biometric certification programs and how they differ: Biometric Component Certification (biometric authentication of users) and Face Verification under Identity Verification (matching a user's selfie to a trusted ID document). Both assess accuracy, liveness/spoof resistance and demographic bias. The page says Face Verification involves at least 10,000 tests and reports IAPAR, FRR and FAR. It lists benefits for vendors (including delta and derivative certification for minor updates) and relying parties, explains that labs are audited and trained by the Biometric Secretariat, and contrasts FIDO certification with NIST FRTE and DHS RIVTD evaluations, which do not result in a certificate. It ends with the ISO standards the programs follow (ISO/IEC 19795 parts, 30107-3/-4, 2382-37, 17025) and the FIDO annexes, as of the snapshot published for 30107-4 and 19795-9 and in development for 19795-10.
