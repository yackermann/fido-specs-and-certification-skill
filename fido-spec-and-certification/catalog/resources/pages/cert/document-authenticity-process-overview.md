# Process Overview for DocAuth Certification

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/identity-verification/document-authenticity/process-overview/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read document-authenticity-process-overview`)

## Summary

Process overview for Document Authenticity (DocAuth) certification of remote/mobile document verification solutions. The programme stands alone: no other FIDO certification is needed first. Five steps: sign the NDA and tell the Identity Verification (IDV) Secretariat you intend to certify; engage a FIDO Accredited DocAuth Laboratory and report the lab and expected timeline to the secretariat; agree a test plan with the lab, have the secretariat approve it, then the lab tests and sends its evaluation report for secretariat approval; file the certification request; and pay the invoiced fee, after which the certificate is issued and listed in the certified products database.
