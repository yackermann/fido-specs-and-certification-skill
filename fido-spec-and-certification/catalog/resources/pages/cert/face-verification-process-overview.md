# Process Overview for Face Verification Certification

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/identity-verification/face-verification/process-overview/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read face-verification-process-overview`)

## Summary

Process overview for Face Verification certification of "selfie match" remote identity verification solutions. It is independent of other FIDO programmes and has no prerequisites. The five steps match the DocAuth flow: NDA and notice of intent to the Identity Verification (IDV) Secretariat; engaging a FIDO Accredited Face Verification Laboratory and reporting the lab and timeline; secretariat-approved test plan, lab testing and secretariat review of the lab report; certification request; and fee invoice, payment and listing of the certificate.
