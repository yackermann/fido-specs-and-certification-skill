# Get Certified for DocAuth

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/identity-verification/document-authenticity/get-certified/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read document-authenticity-get-certified`)

## Summary

A short three-step start guide for Document Authenticity (DocAuth) certification. First, sign the FIDO certification NDA and email it, with your intent to certify, to the IDV Secretariat (idv-secretariat@fidoalliance.org). Second, contact the accredited DocAuth labs and choose one. Third, tell the secretariat which lab you chose and the estimated test timeline, then follow the certification process.
