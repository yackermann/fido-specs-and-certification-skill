# Device Onboard (FDO) Certification Program

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/fido-device-onboard/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read fido-device-onboard`)

## Summary

Overview of the FIDO Device Onboard (FDO) Certification Program for edge and IoT onboarding implementations, open to members and non-members (members get fee discounts). Three implementation classes are certifiable: Device Onboarding Services, FDO-enabled Devices, and Rendezvous Servers. Functional Certification is required for all three; Security Certification applies to DO Services and Devices. As of the snapshot, certification is offered against FDO 1.1. A new specification version becomes available for certification once a first interoperability event can be held, which needs two implementations per class from different companies. Policies and requirements are versioned with sunset dates, and full details live in the policy and requirements documents and the FDO certification process page.
