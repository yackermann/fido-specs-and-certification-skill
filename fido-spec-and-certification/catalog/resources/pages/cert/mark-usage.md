# FIDO Certification Marks Usage Guidelines

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/mark-usage/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read mark-usage`)

## Summary

Explains how owners of FIDO Certified implementations get permission to use the FIDO Certified mark and logo. They sign the Trademark License Agreement (a Japanese copy is offered for reference only) and send it to the Certification Secretariat, then receive the logo package. Misuse of the mark can be reported through a violation form, giving the URL and an image of the misuse.
