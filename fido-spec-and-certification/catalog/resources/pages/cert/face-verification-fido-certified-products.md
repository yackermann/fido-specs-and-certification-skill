# FIDO Certified IDV Face Verification Products

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/identity-verification/face-verification/fido-certified-products/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read face-verification-fido-certified-products`)

## Summary

The public register of identity-verification products certified by FIDO. Each entry gives the product and software version, component, requirements and policy versions, level and reference type, document types, the accredited lab, the measured error rates (FAR/FRR/IAPAR, or DFAR/DFRR for document tests) and the certificate expiry date.

As of the snapshot there are eight entries (Aware, Candour, Northern Point Technology, Veriff, iProov), certified against Biometrics Requirements v4.0 or v4.1 and Policy v1.5, mostly at Level 1, by BixeLab, TÜV IT or Ingenium. Despite the page title, two entries (one from Veriff, one from NPT) are Document Authenticity certifications against DocAuth Requirements v1.1/v1.2. Several cells also contain stray links to unrelated requirement versions.

- Which IDV face products are certified, with their error rates and labs → FIDO® Certified IDV Face Verification Products `#fido-certified-idv-face-verification-products`

- Example DocAuth entries with document lists → Veriff `#veriff`, Northern Point Technology II Private Limited `#northern-point-technology-ii-private-limited-1`
