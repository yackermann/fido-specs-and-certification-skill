# FIDO Certification Fees

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/fido-certification-fees/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read fido-certification-fees`)
Same content also at: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/user-authentication-certification-registration-fees/


## Summary

Central fee schedule for all FIDO certification programmes, with separate member and non-member prices (the page lists the amounts, as of the snapshot). Common terms: fees are charged per certified implementation, cheaper derivative and delta certifications exist for related products, and payment is due before the certificate is issued.

Tables cover user authentication (authenticator and server functional certification, L1+ and higher security levels, derivatives, deltas, on-demand interop testing), Document Authenticity, Biometric Component, FDO (device, onboarding service and rendezvous server) and Face Verification. L1 for authenticators and for FDO devices carries no extra charge beyond the functional certification fee. Lab evaluation costs are not on this page; labs bill separately.

**Look here for:**
- Authenticator, server and security-level fees → User Authentication Certification Fees `#user-authentication-certification-fees`
- Identity verification fees → DocAuth Certification Fees `#docauth-certification-fees`, Face Verification Certification Fees `#face-verification-certification-fees`
- Biometric component fees → Biometric Certification Fees `#biometric-certification-fees`
- FDO device and server fees → FDO Certification Fees `#fdo-certification-fees`
