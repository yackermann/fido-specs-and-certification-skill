# Laboratory Accreditation Process - Biometrics

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/accreditation-process-fees-biometrics/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read accreditation-process-fees-biometrics`)
Same content also at: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/biometric-component-certification/fido-accredited-biometric-laboratories/


## Summary

Explains how a laboratory becomes a FIDO Accredited Biometric Laboratory; the binding rules are in the Biometric Laboratory Accreditation Policy. Steps: review the policy and third-party prerequisites, send the application form to the biometric secretariat, hold an on-boarding call, work through the secretariat's technical and legal review, have every biometric tester attend training and pass an exam, then pay fees and set up evaluator accounts.

Requirements are ISO/IEC 17025 compliance (via an existing accreditation, a national body, or shown directly to FIDO) plus competence in biometric performance testing (ISO/IEC 19795-1) and presentation attack detection testing (ISO 30107 Part 3; the table dates it 2007, which looks like an error). NIST NVLAP biometric scopes or a Common Criteria licence can serve as evidence, but CC only counts with a listed biometric Protection Profile evaluation or CAFVM/BEAT use. Fees are on a separate page.
