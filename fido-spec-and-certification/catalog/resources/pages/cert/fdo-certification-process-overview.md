# FDO Certification Process Overview

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/fdo-certification-process-overview/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read fdo-certification-process-overview`)

## Summary

Step-by-step guide to FIDO Device Onboard (FDO) certification, a programme separate from the other FIDO programmes, for three implementation classes: Device Onboarding Services, FDO-enabled devices and Rendezvous Servers. The seven steps are: application form plus certification NDA; self-run conformance testing; interoperability testing; security evaluation via one L1 Vendor Questionnaire per product, emailed to the Security Secretariat (Rendezvous Servers are exempt); certification request and invoicing; certificate issuance after payment, with an optional trademark licence and a short window to correct certificate details; and ongoing maintenance.

Maintenance differs by class. Rendezvous Servers only have a derivative route for unchanged components, illustrated with scenario examples. For DO services and devices the vendor files an FDO Impact Analysis Report, and the Security Secretariat rates the change non-interfering, minor or major, which leads to derivative, delta (re-run conformance and interop) or full recertification.
