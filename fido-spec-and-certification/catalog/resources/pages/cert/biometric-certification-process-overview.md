# Biometric Component Certification Program

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/biometric-certification-process-overview/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read biometric-certification-process-overview`)

## Summary

Walks biometric subsystem vendors through the Biometric Component Certification process. The programme stands alone, with no other FIDO certification required first. A certified component is optional for L1 and L2 authenticators, but mandatory at L3 and above if biometrics are used. The vendor writes an Allowed Integration Document that sets the rules for building the component into an authenticator.

The steps are as follows. Request an account and apply through the Biometric Dashboard, where the secretariat reviews the application. Then submit the component and documents to an accredited biometric lab, agreeing the cost and timeline with that lab. The lab runs online and offline live-subject testing and reports to the vendor and the secretariat, who approves or rejects. Next, file a certification request including metadata (certification level, self-attested FAR and FRR), and the certificate is issued. Publishing that metadata to the MDS afterwards is optional.
