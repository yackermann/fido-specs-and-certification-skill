# FIDO Certification

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/fido-certification/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read fido-certification`)

## Summary

The FIDO Alliance's top-level certification hub. It helps vendors pick the right programme and points buyers to the certified products directory. A table maps each programme to what it tests and which products it covers: Biometric Component, Face Verification (IDV), Document Authenticity (DocAuth), FIDO Device Onboard (FDO), User Authentication Functional (UAF, U2F, FIDO2 authenticators, clients and servers) and Authenticator Security Levels. Each programme has its own process. Fees are on a separate fees page. Programmes are open to non-members, but members get testing discounts and co-marketing.

The resources section links the certified product listings (general, FDO and IDV Face; as of the snapshot the general list is described as holding more than 1,200 implementations), the Certified Showcase for finding vendors, and the accredited laboratory locator.
