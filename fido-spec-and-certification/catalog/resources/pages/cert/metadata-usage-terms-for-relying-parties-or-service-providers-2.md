# Metadata Usage Terms (for Relying Parties or Service Providers)

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/metadata-usage-terms-for-relying-parties-or-service-providers-2/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read metadata-usage-terms-for-relying-parties-or-service-providers-2`)

## Summary

Legal terms for relying parties that download Metadata Statements and the BLOB from the FIDO Metadata Service (MDS v3.0). Use is limited to enabling FIDO authentication. Users must keep legal headers intact and verify the BLOB signature before use. They must not use an invalid BLOB, and must delete statements that have dropped out of the latest BLOB. The terms also cover ownership, disclaimers, liability limits and indemnification.
