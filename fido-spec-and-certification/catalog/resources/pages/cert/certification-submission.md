# User Authentication Certification Registration

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/functional-certification/certification-submission/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read certification-submission`)

## Summary

Lists what to submit for user-authentication (functional) certification once both conformance and interoperability testing have passed. You need the Certification Registration Form, conformance self-validation results, interoperability results, the FIDO Vendor Self-Assertion Checklist and the certification fees. Processing time varies and depends on payment arriving. Relying parties using FIDO Certified servers may display the UAF, U2F or FIDO2 logos under FIDO's relying-party logo terms. Note that the derivative-certification page links an older version of the self-assertion checklist.
