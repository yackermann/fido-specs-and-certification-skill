# Document Authenticity Certification (DocAuth)

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/identity-verification/document-authenticity/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read document-authenticity`)

## Summary

A mostly promotional overview of the FIDO Document Authenticity (DocAuth) certification, one of the two identity-verification programmes (the other is Face Verification). It is aimed at vendors of remote ID-document verification. The certification checks whether a remote solution can tell if an in-scope government document is genuine: ID cards, driving licences, passports, residence permits, visas, voter cards and similar. The threats tested are counterfeits, physical and digital tampering, forgery, expired or invalidated documents, presentation attacks and face morphs. FIDO Accredited Laboratories do the testing. Certified products are listed in FIDO's certified-products database and receive a certificate and access to the logo. The page has no process steps, requirement versions or fees; it links to a separate Get Certified page.
