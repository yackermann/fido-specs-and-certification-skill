# FIDO User Authentication Certification Programs

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/fido-user-authentication-certification-programs/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read fido-user-authentication-certification-programs`)

## Summary

An overview of FIDO's passkey (user authentication) certification programmes, aimed at vendors and at businesses deploying passkeys. It lists what can be certified. Hardware authenticators (FIDO2, U2F) and software authenticators (UAF, FIDO2) must reach at least Authenticator Level 1 and may go higher. Servers (FIDO2, UAF) get functional certification only. Most of the page lists benefits for each audience. It ends with links to functional certification for authenticators and clients, functional certification for servers, and Biometric Component Certification. Note that the page calls the biometric programme an identity-verification test, while other pages present it as a component programme for authenticators.
