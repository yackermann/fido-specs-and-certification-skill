# Get Certified for Face Verification

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/identity-verification/face-verification/get-certified/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read face-verification-get-certified`)

## Summary

Short starting page for Face Verification (remote identity verification) certification. It gives three steps: complete the FIDO vendor NDA, email the signed NDA with a statement of intent to the Identity Verification secretariat address on the page, then pick a lab from the FIDO accredited Face Verification lab list and contact it to start testing.
