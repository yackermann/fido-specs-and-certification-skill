# FIDO Accredited Biometric Laboratories

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/biometric-component-introduction/accredited-labs/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read biometric-component-introduction-accredited-labs`)

## Summary

Page about laboratories accredited for Biometric Component Certification and how a lab becomes one. In the snapshot text the lab list itself is empty apart from a note that applications are open, so the actual list was not captured. Accreditation steps: review the biometric lab accreditation policy, submit the application form to the Biometric Secretariat, an onboarding call, a joint review of technical and legal fitness, training with an exam for every tester, then fee payment and evaluator account setup.

Requirements are ISO/IEC 17025 compliance plus demonstrated competence in biometric performance and PAD testing; existing NIST NVLAP biometric accreditation or Common Criteria licensing (with a biometric Protection Profile evaluation, or CAFVM/BEAT evidence) can serve as proof. The page lists accreditation, yearly and three-yearly renewal fees as of the snapshot. Its ISO edition references (19795-1:2006, 30107 dated 2007) are older than those cited in the Biometric Certification FAQ.
