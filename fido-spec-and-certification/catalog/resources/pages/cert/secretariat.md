# Certification Program Secretariats

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/certification/secretariat/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read secretariat`)

## Summary

Describes the four secretariats that run FIDO's certification programmes and gives a contact email for each. The Certification Secretariat handles overall delivery of all programmes (authenticators, servers, passkey providers, biometrics, identity verification, FDO), including test oversight, lab management, certificates, invoicing and trademarks. The Security Secretariat manages security certification for authenticators and FDO devices/onboarding services, reviews vendor questionnaires and liaises with security labs. The Biometric Secretariat runs Biometric Component Certification, and the Identity Verification Secretariat runs the Document Authenticity and Face Verification programmes; both validate lab reports and coordinate with their accredited labs.

**Look here for:**
- Who to email about general certification, certificates or invoices? → Certification Secretariat `#certification-secretariat`
- Who reviews security vendor questionnaires? → Security Secretariat `#security-secretariat`
- Who handles biometric component evaluations? → Biometric Secretariat `#biometric-secretariat`
- Who handles DocAuth or Face Verification? → Identity Verification Secretariat `#identity-verification-secretariat`
