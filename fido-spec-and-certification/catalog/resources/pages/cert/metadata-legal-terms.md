# MDS Legal Terms

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/metadata-legal-terms/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read metadata-legal-terms`)

## Summary

Lists the three legal agreements behind the Metadata Service (submission terms for authenticator vendors, usage terms for developers, usage terms for relying parties/service providers), each in HTML and PDF, and notes that legal headers are carried in the BLOB and every metadata statement. Vendors accept their terms when registering to submit; developers and RPs are deemed to accept theirs through continued use of MDS.
