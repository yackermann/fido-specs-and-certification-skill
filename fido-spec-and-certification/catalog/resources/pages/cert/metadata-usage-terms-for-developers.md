# Metadata Usage Terms (for Developers)

Source: https://fidoalliance.org/metadata-usage-terms-for-developers/ (live text: `python3 scripts/resources.py read metadata-usage-terms-for-developers`)

## Summary

Legal terms that bind software developers who fetch the MDS BLOB and the Metadata Statements in it (MDS v3.0 formats); use is limited to enabling FIDO authentication. Among the obligations: keep legal headers intact, verify the BLOB signature and never use an invalid BLOB, delete statements that drop out of the latest BLOB, and pass equivalent minimum terms on to downstream licensees, pointing them to the separate Relying Party / Service Provider usage terms.
