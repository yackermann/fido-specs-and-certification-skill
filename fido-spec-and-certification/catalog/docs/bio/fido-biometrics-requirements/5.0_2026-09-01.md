# FIDO Biometrics Requirements

Version 5.0 · Review Draft 02 · 2026-09-01 · **ACTIVE** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/biometric/requirements/Biometrics-Requirements-v5.0-rd02-20260901.html)

## Summary

The FIDO Biometrics Requirements define the performance and presentation-attack criteria, and the laboratory test procedures, for two FIDO biometric certification programs: the Biometric Component for FIDO authenticators (BCC; the abstract calls it BCA) and Face Verification for Remote Identity Verification (IdV Face). FIDO Accredited Biometric Laboratories run online and offline tests and measure FAR, FRR and IAPAR. For BCC the results are graded as BioLevel 1, 1+, 2 or 2+, which differ in test crew size (25 or 245 subjects), error-rate thresholds and mandatory self-attestation. The document also sets test crew demographics, PAI species triage by attack potential, rate limits, injection-attack testing for IdV Face, an optional differential-performance evaluation across demographic groups, and what the lab report and FIDO metadata must contain.

The process around these tests (application, qualification, lab report, issuance) is in the Biometric Component Certification Policy. A certified BCC can be used inside an authenticator going through FIDO Authenticator Certification. The file is labelled Review Draft 02, but FIDO lists v5.0 as active for certification.

**Who needs it:** biometric and authenticator vendors, IdV Face vendors, accredited biometric labs, certification applicants

**Look here for:**
- Which FAR/FRR/IAPAR thresholds and subject counts apply to each BioLevel? → §3.1.1 `#CertCriteria`
- What extra requirements apply to claim NIST SP 800-63-4 alignment? → §3.7 `#Req_800-63A`, §3.7.1 `#Req_800-63B`
- How many subjects are needed, and with what age, gender and skin-tone mix? → §5.1.1 `#NumberofSubjects`, §5.1.2 `#Population`
- Which presentation attack instruments must be tested at Level A/B? → §6.2.4 `#PAISpecies`, Appendix A `#TriagePAD`
- What goes into the lab test report and the FIDO metadata? → §7 `#TestReporting`, §7.1.5 `#FIDOMetadata`

**Changes from previous version:** Adds requirements for aligning with NIST SP 800-63-4. §3.7 covers IdV Face against 800-63A-4, including a 1:N false positive identification threshold and gallery-size rule. §3.7.1 covers BCC against 800-63B-4: PAD is required for face and voice matching is excluded. Matching additions appear in the differential-performance section (§3.6.1.1–3.6.1.2). Skin-tone grouping of the test crew moves from the Monk scale to the Colorimetric Skin Tone Scale, and self-reported tone is accepted. IdV Face Level 2 becomes much stricter: at least 245 subjects instead of 100, FAR below 1:10,000 instead of 1:3000, and FRR below 1:100 instead of 5:100 (upper bounds of 80% confidence intervals). Level 2 now meets the NIST 800-63A-4 thresholds.

## Reading guide

**Start here:** the two level tables, §3.1.1 `#CertCriteria` (BCC BioLevels) and §3.2 `#Requirements_IdVFace` (IdV Face levels per Reference Type), give subject counts and thresholds in one view. Then read §3.3 `#TOE` to see what the vendor must hand over and §4 `#TestHarness` for the harness functions. Throughout the document, a parent section holds the common rule and "- BCC" / "- IdV Face" subsections hold the program-specific numbers. Always read both.

**Section by section:**
- §2 Introduction `#intro`, §2.1 `#intro_BCA`, §2.2 `#intro_IdV-Face`, §2.7 `#BioTerms`: scope is matching plus PAD for both programs. For IdV Face, document authenticity belongs to the separate DocAuth program. The verification attempt vs. transaction definitions in §2.7 drive every rate calculation.
- §3.1.1 `#CertCriteria`: BioLevel 1/1+/2/2+. The "+" levels use 245 FAR/FRR subjects instead of 25, FAR 0.01% instead of 1% and FRR 5% instead of 7%. Levels 1 and 2 differ only in IAPAR (15% vs 7%). L1 and L2 require documented self-attestation of FAR ≤1:10,000 and FRR ≤5%; for the "+" levels it is optional. PAD always uses 15 subjects with 6 Level A and 8 Level B species.
- §3.2 `#Requirements_IdVFace`: Level 1 (25 subjects, FAR 1%, FRR 7%) and Level 2 (245 subjects, FAR 0.01%, FRR 1%), each tested separately for Reference Type 1 (photo of the document) and Type 2 (chip/NFC image). IAPAR has two limits: 7% per species and 4% across all species.
- §3.3 Target of Evaluation `#TOE`, §3.3.1 `#TOE-BCC`, §3.3.2 `#TOE-IdVFace`: the TOE SHALL cover capture through decision and is delivered as a Common Test Harness. The Allowed Integration Document must match the harness. BCC: configurations spanning the integration range (for example glass thickness) are tested, and fusion TOEs must declare parallel or sequential collection. IdV Face: one TOE per allowed integration and per reference input type.
- §3.3.3 `#TOE_software_only`, §3.3.4 `#testing-remote-subjects`: local vs remote mode per ISO 27553. A biometric inside a FIDO authenticator must run in local mode. Software-only TOEs come as a container under exclusive lab control, with no data returned to the vendor. Remote subjects are allowed with approved capture devices and recorded, observed sessions.
- §3.4 Performance Levels `#Performance` through §3.4.7 `#FAR-MT`: transactions are defined by the Allowed Integration Document (SHOULD NOT exceed 30 s). FRR counts mated transactions rejected or failing to acquire on every attempt. FAR comes from offline zero-effort impostor comparisons, which ignore FTA. Both use one fixed vendor threshold and the upper bound of an 80% confidence interval. Self-attestation tiers run from 1:10,000 to 1:100,000. §3.4.7 gives the FAR formula for several interchangeable fingers or eyes (`FARMT` ≤1:10,000); without that analysis, one reference is the maximum.
- §3.5 PAD Criteria `#PADCriteria`, §3.5.1 `#IAPAR`, §3.5.2 `#RateLimits`, §3.5.3 `#InjectionAttacks`: IAPAR is computed per PAI species; an FTA on an attack is not an error. Rate limiting SHOULD be off during testing. BCC rate limits are checked in Authenticator Certification, while IdV Face SHALL limit verification to 5 transactions. Injection-attack defence for IdV Face is documentation the lab checks, not a penetration test, plus a NIST 800-63A media-analysis requirement on the vendor.
- §3.6 Differential Performance `#Requirement_DiffPerf`, §3.6.1 `#DiffPerfReq` (optional): FRR only, below 6% for each age, gender and skin-tone group, at the certification operating point. BCC can use it only at 1+/2+; IdV Face needs 245 subjects. §3.6.1.1 `#DiffPerfReq_80063A` and §3.6.1.2 `#DiffPerfReq_80063B` add the NIST demographic FMR rules, each met through NIST FRTE results, extra lab subjects or documented self-attestation.
- §3.7 `#Req_800-63A`, §3.7.1 `#Req_800-63B`: optional NIST 800-63-4 alignment. For IdV Face this means periodic independent testing and, when 1:N is used, FPIR ≤1:1,000 at FRR ≤1% on a gallery of at least 90% of operational size. For BCC it means PAD SHALL be present for face (SHOULD for iris and fingerprint), voice SHALL NOT be used, and FNMR SHOULD be below 5%.
- §4 Common Test Harness `#TestHarness`, §4.1 `#TestHarness_BCC`, §4.2 `#TestHarness_IDV_Face`, §4.3 `#ToeSecurityGuidelines`: for each operating point the vendor provides enrollment, online verification that emits stored verification transactions, offline batch matching and logging. Harness data SHALL be encrypted and authenticated with algorithms from the Allowed Cryptography List.
- §5.1 Test Crew `#TestCrew` (§5.1.1 `#NumberofSubjects`, §5.1.2 `#Population`): age bands 18–30, 31–50 and 51+ at 25–40% each, nobody under 18; gender 40–60% each; CST groups 1–3, 4–6 and 7–10 at 25–40% each. Up to four fingers or two eyes per person may count as subjects, but at least 123 unique persons are needed, and one extra subject is enrolled per FTE. Twins SHOULD be excluded.
- §5.1.3 Statistics `#TestSize`: bootstrap with at least 1,000 samples for FAR and FRR, plus a zero-error fallback (§5.1.3.3 `#Ruleof3FAR`, §5.1.3.4 `#Ruleof3FRR`). §5.1.5 `#TestEnviron` to §5.1.7 `#EnrollmentReq`: every environment configuration is tested; reference adaptation is trained and then switched off. IdV document capture needs at least 90 pixels between the eyes and a vendor-neutral capture API.
- §5.2 Test Methods `#TestMethods`: FTE is declared after three failed enrollment transactions. Each subject performs 10 mated transactions with deliberate variability. §5.2.3.1 `#OfflineSoftwareVal` requires offline replay to reproduce the online decision sequence exactly. Impostor comparisons between fingers or eyes of the same person are excluded.
- §5.3 Documented Self-Attestation `#SelfAttestation`, §5.3.1 `#SelfAttestationProcedures`: the vendor's own test at a chosen FAR tier, with a subject-count table, run on the same system and operating point. The lab accepts the claim only if its bootstrap mean is at or below the claimed value.
- §6 PAD procedures `#ProceduresPAD`: 15 subjects (§6.1.1 `#NumberofSubjectsPAD`), one PAI per species per subject, 10 attack transactions per PAI (§6.2.5.1 `#ImpostorPADTransactions`), so 150 per species and at most 10 accepts for 7%. Four of the eight Level B species SHALL be tailored to the TOE's technology (§6.2.5 `#PAI`), and the secretariat approves the selection. Each PAI is quality-checked before use. §6.2.6 `#MultiBiometric_Testing_for_PAD` covers fusion.
- §7 Test Reporting `#TestReporting`: the vendor sees the report before FIDO and only the vendor may share it further. The lab keeps the full interaction log and does not send it to FIDO or the vendor. §7.2 `#FarFrrReportingReqs` and §7.3 `#PadReportingReqs` follow ISO 19795-1 and 30107-3/-4 item lists.
- Appendix A `#TriagePAD`: attack potential collapsed into Levels A, B and C (time, expertise, equipment, access to the characteristic), with example species per modality (`#PAISpeciesFingerprint`, `#PAISpeciesFace`, `#PAISpeciesIrisEye`, `#PAISpeciesVoice`). Face includes deepfake videos at A and B. Level C is described but never selected for testing.

**What matters most:**
- One fixed operating point per evaluation (§3.4.2 `#FRRReq`, §3.4.3 `#FARReq`, §3.5.1 `#IAPAR`). FRR, FAR and IAPAR all use the vendor's threshold for the claimed FAR, and each extra operating point means another evaluation (and fee, per the policy).
- The transaction definition in the Allowed Integration Document (§3.4.1 `#VerTrans`) sets how many attempts count before a reject. It affects FRR and IAPAR directly and cannot change mid-test.
- Offline/online equivalence (§5.2.3.1 `#OfflineSoftwareVal`): FAR is computed with the vendor's offline SDK, so a harness whose offline decisions differ from online ones fails before any rate is calculated.
- For BioLevel 1/2 and IdV Level 1, self-attestation is mandatory (§3.4.5 `#Self-AttestationforFAR`). If the lab's bootstrap mean exceeds the claim, the value is kept out of metadata.
- Test crew demographics (§5.1.2 `#Population`) are SHALL targets. Deviations need secretariat agreement, so recruitment planning (including the 123-person floor) often decides the schedule.
- Left to the vendor and lab, with secretariat approval: the variability factors for mated transactions, the PAI species and tailored Level B attacks, the environments to test, and the IdV document-type mix.

**Watch out for:**
- Review Draft text with leftovers. §5.3.1 `#SelfAttestationProcedures` still lists FAR tiers ending in "or others?", and §2.1 `#intro_BCA` says BCA where the rest says BCC.
- The "Rule of 3" sections (§5.1.3.3 `#Ruleof3FAR`, §5.1.3.4 `#Ruleof3FRR`) actually compute the 80% bound with 1.61/N. The 3/N figures in the table are the 95% version.
- §3.4.2.1 `#FRRReq-BCC` phrases self-attested FRR as "at least 5%" where the table in §3.1.1 `#CertCriteria` means at most 5%. §3.4.3 `#FARReq` and §5.3.1 talk about a 1:10,000 FIDO requirement although BioLevel 1/2 and IdV Level 1 test FAR at 1%.
- §3.4.6.2 `#Self-AttestationforFRR_IdVFace` says IdV has only one FRR level, but §3.2 sets 7% and 1%. §5.1.3.4 compares IdV Level 2 against 5%, not 1%.
- Self-attestation is headed "BCC Only" (§5.3 `#SelfAttestation`), yet it is mandatory for IdV Level 1 (§3.4.5.2 `#Self-AttestationforFAR_IdVFace`).
- §3.1.1 says BCC Level 2 meets the NIST 800-63A-4 thresholds, though 800-63A is the identity-proofing volume the IdV sections use. Treat the BCC mapping to 800-63B-4 in §3.7.1 `#Req_800-63B` as the authoritative one.
- §7.2 sends the FAR/FRR report to FIDO, while §7.3 addresses the PAD report "to the vendor"; the policy expects one Laboratory Evaluation Report to both.
- Long passages copied from ISO/IEC 19795-1 (§5.2.2.2 `#OnlineMated`) mix lower-case "shall/should" with FIDO's capitalised keywords.
