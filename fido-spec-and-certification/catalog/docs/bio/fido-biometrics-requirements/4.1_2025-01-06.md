# FIDO Biometrics Requirements

Version 4.1 · Final Document · 2025-01-06 · **ACTIVE until 2027-03-31** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/biometric/requirements/Biometrics-Requirements-v4.1-fd-20250106.html)

## Summary

v4.1 of the FIDO Biometrics Requirements covers the same two programs as v4.0: the Biometric Component for FIDO authenticators (BCC) and Face Verification for Remote Identity Verification (IdV Face). It defines the FAR/FRR/IAPAR criteria per BioLevel, test crew rules, PAD test methods and lab reporting. FIDO lists it as active until 2027-03-31. According to its revision history it is a maintenance release over v4.0, which is not catalogued. It adds a 1:10,000 self-attested FAR for BioLevels 1 and 2, fixes BioLevel inconsistencies and the transaction count, and edits voice PAD. Compared with v5.0, it still groups test crew skin tone on the Monk scale and has no NIST SP 800-63-4 alignment section.

**Who needs it:** biometric and authenticator vendors, IdV Face vendors, accredited biometric labs, certification applicants
