# FIDO Biometrics Certification Policy

Version 1.5 · Final Document · 2024-05-22 · **ACTIVE** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/biometric/certificationpolicy/Biometric-Component-Certification-Policy-v1.5-fd-20240522.html)

## Summary

The Biometric Component Certification Policy sets out how FIDO runs its biometric certification programs: the Biometric Component program (BCC) and, since v1.5, Face Verification for Remote Identity Verification (IdV Face). It covers the steps from application to certificate. The vendor applies and contracts an accredited lab. At qualification the vendor gives the lab the TOE and test harness, a TOE Description, a Test Plan and, for BCC, an Allowed Integration Document. The lab tests against the Biometrics Requirements and submits a Laboratory Evaluation Report. The vendor then sends a certification request with fees, charged per evaluated operating point for BCC. No other FIDO certification is a prerequisite.

The policy also defines how a certified component can be used in FIDO Authenticator Certification. A certified biometric is optional at L1/L2, and any biometric used for authentication at L3 or above must be a certified component. It also covers which biometric data reaches the Metadata Service, Delta and Derivative certification after changes, certificate states (active, certified, suspended, revoked), and disputes. Versioning rules require every certification to target an active version of the Biometrics Requirements. A new version becomes active on its Evaluation Availability Date, and the previous version is phased out over a transition period that ends at its Sunset Date.

**Who needs it:** biometric component and IdV Face vendors, authenticator vendors integrating a certified biometric, accredited biometric labs, certification applicants
