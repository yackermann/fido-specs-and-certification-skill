# FIDO Biometrics Certification Policy

Version 1.5 · Final Document · 2024-05-22 · **ACTIVE** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/biometric/certificationpolicy/Biometric-Component-Certification-Policy-v1.5-fd-20240522.html)

## Summary

The Biometric Component Certification Policy sets out how FIDO runs its biometric certification programs: the Biometric Component program (BCC) and, since v1.5, Face Verification for Remote Identity Verification (IdV Face). It covers the steps from application to certificate. The vendor applies and contracts an accredited lab. At qualification the vendor gives the lab the TOE and test harness, a TOE Description, a Test Plan and, for BCC, an Allowed Integration Document. The lab tests against the Biometrics Requirements and submits a Laboratory Evaluation Report. The vendor then sends a certification request with fees, charged per evaluated operating point for BCC. No other FIDO certification is a prerequisite.

The policy also defines how a certified component can be used in FIDO Authenticator Certification. A certified biometric is optional at L1/L2, and any biometric used for authentication at L3 or above must be a certified component. It also covers which biometric data reaches the Metadata Service, Delta and Derivative certification after changes, certificate states (active, certified, suspended, revoked), and disputes. Versioning rules require every certification to target an active version of the Biometrics Requirements. A new version becomes active on its Evaluation Availability Date, and the previous version is phased out over a transition period that ends at its Sunset Date.

**Who needs it:** biometric component and IdV Face vendors, authenticator vendors integrating a certified biometric, accredited biometric labs, certification applicants

## Reading guide

**Start here:** §3.4 Biometric Certification Process `#BioProcess` and its subsections give the six steps from application to issuance. Read §3.2 `#FIDOCerts` if the component will end up in a FIDO Certified authenticator, and §3.17.2.2 `#ActiveVersion` to know which Biometrics Requirements version you can test against. The test content itself is not here; it is in `fido-biometrics-requirements`.

**Section by section:**
- §2 Introduction `#intro` and §2.2–2.4 (`#Roles`, `#FIDOTerms`, `#PersonnelDefs`): two programs, BCC and IdV Face, with separate secretariats (Biometric and Identity Verification) and lab types. Up to four fingers or both eyes of one person may count as separate test subjects.
- §3.1 Prerequisite Certifications `#Preqs`: no other FIDO certification is needed first. Non-members can apply.
- §3.2 `#FIDOCerts` (BCC): the component MUST be integrated as its Allowed Integration Document says. The authenticator needs Authenticator Certification at L1 or higher; a Functional Certificate alone is not enough. Optional at L1/L2; at L3 and above any biometric used for authentication MUST be a certified component.
- §3.3 `#FIDOCerts①` and §3.3.1 `#BioSusystem` (IdV Face): scope is face matching against a document photo plus PAD. Document authenticity belongs to the separate DocAuth program. The vendor sets the TOE boundary, which must include capture, signal processing, storage, comparison and decision.
- §3.4.1 Application `#Application` and §3.4.2 Qualification `#Qualification`: the vendor contracts an accredited lab and SHALL hand over TOE, harness, TOE Description, Test Plan and (BCC) Allowed Integration Document. The lab SHALL check completeness, SHOULD pre-test with at least one performance transaction and one PAD attempt, and submits the documents to the secretariat. Qualification does not confirm vendor-claimed FAR/FRR/PAD numbers.
- §3.4.3 Biometric Testing `#BioTesting`, §3.4.3.1 `#AllowedIntegrationDocument`, §3.4.3.2 `#BiometricData`: online and offline live-subject testing per the Requirements, with a FIDO-approved lab test plan. The Allowed Integration Document MUST list permitted hardware and software changes. Subject data stays with the lab. It may go to the vendor under contract, but never to FIDO or other third parties except for lab audits.
- §3.4.4 Laboratory Evaluation Report `#LabReport`: results for every requirement, the lab's validation that allowed integration changes do not affect performance, and vendor self-attestation, which the lab treats as a checklist only.
- §3.5 `#RequestSteps` and §3.6 `#RequestSteps①`: request contents and fees charged per evaluated operating point (amounts are on the FIDO website).
- §3.7 `#IssuanceSteps①` and §3.8 `#IssuanceSteps②`: certificate fields, including level with IAPAR threshold and test environment, plus the `RRRVVVVPPPDDDDDDDDNNN` number format. IdV Face certificates carry a 3-year expiry.
- §3.8.1 FIDO Metadata Service `#Metadata` (BCC): MDS publishes the certification level, self-attested FAR and FRR, and the certified IAPAR. Publishing to MDS is optional, but metadata MUST be submitted during certification and the lab checks it.
- §3.10 `#PostCertification①`, §3.10.1 `#Delta`, §3.10.2 `#Derivative`, §3.10.2.1 `#DerivativeProcess` (BCC): change table. Changes covered by the Allowed Integration Document need nothing. Minor hardware or software changes need a Delta with an Impact Analysis Report. A major software change on the same sensor needs a Delta retest on the old data, if that data was never given to the vendor. A sensor change, or a hardware change that affects performance, needs New Certification. Derivatives need a vendor assertion approved by the secretariat.
- §3.12 `#PostCertification②` with §3.12.1 `#Delta①` and §3.12.2 `#Derivative①` (IdV Face): 3-year validity with a 6-month grace period and expiry notices. An Impact Analysis Report SHALL be filed for hardware or sensor changes, vulnerabilities or significant software updates. A Delta keeps the original expiry date.
- §3.11 `#CertificationStates`, §3.13 `#CertificationStates①`, §3.14 `#Suspension`, §3.15 `#Revocation`, §3.16 `#Dispute`: Active, Certified, Suspended and Revoked. Suspension comes with at least 30 days' notice and is lifted by a Delta. Revocation follows expiry or more than 180 days in suspension and needs New Certification.
- §3.17 Program Administration `#Admin` and §3.17.1 `#Sensitive`: documents only via website forms (emailed documents are deleted unless agreed), aggregate-only operational reports, and limits on certification claims.
- §3.17.2 `#Require_Versioning`, §3.17.2.1 `#BiometricRequire`, §3.17.2.2 `#ActiveVersion`: certify only against an Active Version of the Requirements. A new version becomes Active on its Evaluation Availability Date. Major and minor changes give the previous version 6 months to sunset; emergency changes sunset it immediately. Applications approved before a major or minor sunset may finish, but emergency changes apply even to evaluations in progress. The CWG votes on sunset dates.

**What matters most:**
- The Allowed Integration Document (§3.4.3.1 `#AllowedIntegrationDocument`, §3.10 `#PostCertification①`) decides what an integrator may change without a Delta, so write it broadly enough at the start.
- L3+ authenticators MUST use a certified component for any biometric used to authenticate (§3.2 `#FIDOCerts`).
- Metadata accuracy is checked by the lab even if the vendor never publishes it to MDS (§3.8.1 `#Metadata`).
- Pick the Requirements version with the sunset rules in mind (§3.17.2.2 `#ActiveVersion`). New and Delta certifications both need an Active Version.

**Watch out for:**
- The heading structure is broken in places: §3.4.5 `#CertRequest` and §3.9 `#PostCertification` are empty, §3.6.1 `#IssuanceSteps` is an empty "Certificate Issuance" heading under the IdV request section, and the BCC-specific MDS section §3.8.1 `#Metadata` sits under the IdV issuance heading. Go by the BCC/IdV labels, not the nesting.
- The versioning example in §3.17.2.2 `#ActiveVersion` says the old version sunsets one year after the new one, but its own table and the change classes give 6 months.
- Expiry and the 3-year renewal are written only for IdV Face (§3.8 `#IssuanceSteps②`, §3.12 `#PostCertification②`). No BCC expiry is stated, yet §3.15 `#Revocation` lists expiry as a revocation event.
- Copy-paste slips: §3.11.4 `#Revoked` says "Authenticator Certificate", and §3.6 `#RequestSteps①` charges IdV Face under "Biometric Component Fees".

**Related documents:** `fido-biometrics-requirements` (the tests, thresholds and lab report content the lab applies). `fido-authenticator-certification-policy` and `fido-authenticator-security-requirements` for integrating a certified component into an L1–L3+ authenticator. `fido-metadata-statement` for how the biometric figures appear in metadata.
