# FIDO Bluetooth® Specification v1.0

Version 1.2 · Proposed Standard · 2017-04-11 · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/fido-u2f-v1.2-ps-20170411/fido-u2f-bt-protocol-v1.2-ps-20170411.html)

## Summary

Specifies how U2F messages travel over Bluetooth Low Energy (titled FIDO Bluetooth Specification v1.0 inside the U2F 1.2 suite). Both sides must conform to Bluetooth Core 4.0 or later. Clients and authenticators must pair, keep a long-term key and encrypt everything, and the authenticator must require LE Security Mode 1 Level 2 or 3 before any U2F traffic. The authenticator exposes the FIDO U2F GATT service (UUID 0xFFFD) with characteristics u2fControlPoint (client writes requests), u2fStatus (notifications carry responses), u2fControlPointLength, u2fServiceRevision and u2fServiceRevisionBitfield, alongside the Device Information and Generic Access services. Requests and responses use a small framing with commands PING, KEEPALIVE (status PROCESSING or TUP_NEEDED), MSG and ERROR, and long messages are fragmented to the control-point length. The document also covers advertising flags for pairing and non-pairing mode, notifications, and client- and authenticator-side guidance on pairing, throughput and address types.

CTAP 2.3 lists this document as superseded by its own BLE binding, which keeps the 0xFFFD service UUID; the U2F version may still be implemented for backward compatibility. U2F 1.2 is the only certifiable U2F version.

**Who needs it:** authenticator vendors (BLE devices), platform & client developers, test labs
