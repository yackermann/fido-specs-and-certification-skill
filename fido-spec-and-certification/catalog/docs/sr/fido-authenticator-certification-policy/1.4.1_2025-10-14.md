# FIDO Authenticator Certification Policy

Version 1.4.1 · Final Document · 2025-10-14 · **latest published (the certification-levels page still links v1.3 PDF)** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/certification/authenticator/authenticator-policy-v1.4.1-fd-20251014.html)

## Summary

The rules of the FIDO Authenticator Certification Program. It covers roles and program documents; the levels L1, L1+, L2, L2+, L3 and L3+, with the L3/L3+ companion programs (GlobalPlatform TEE, Common Criteria and GlobalPlatform FIDO2 SE); and the step-by-step process. That process runs from preparation, through Functional Certification as a prerequisite and the application, to the security evaluation: Vendor Questionnaire, Secretariat review at L1, lab evaluation at L1+ and above, then report review and issuance. It also covers maintenance through the FIDO Impact Analysis Report (derivative, delta, re-certification), suspension and revocation, and vulnerability handling with per-level attack-potential calculations and vendor deadlines. Under §10.5, a new version of the security test procedures becomes active on its availability date and the previous one sunsets after 1 year for a major change, 6 months for a minor one, or immediately in an emergency.

Its revision history lists one change in v1.4.1 (2025-10-14) from v1.4.0: the §10.5.6 exception. CTAP 2.1 products certified against SR v1.5.1 at L3/L3+ may use the GlobalPlatform FIDO2 SE companion program with no sunset date. Status: latest published version. FIDO's certification-levels page still links the v1.3 PDF.

**Who needs it:** certification applicants and authenticator vendors, FIDO-accredited security labs

## Reading guide

**Start here:**
- §5 `#AuthenticatorLevels`: what each level means and who evaluates it.
- §6.1.1 `#ProcessSteps`: the step table with the responsible party for each step.
- §7.1 `#FIAR`: how changes to a certified product are handled.
- §10.5.4 `#TestProceduresActive`: which SR / Test Procedure versions you may certify against.

**Section by section:**
- §2.3 `#FunctionalCert` and §6.3 `#FunctionalStep`: Functional Certification (conformance self-validation plus interoperability testing) must be complete before you apply.
- §3 `#RolesResponsibilities`:
  - CWG owns the policy, and SPWG writes the requirements.
  - The Certification Secretariat runs operations; the Security Secretariat reviews questionnaires and reports and monitors threats.
  - The Crisis Response Team handles vulnerabilities; the Board Certification Committee resolves certification issues.
  - Accredited labs perform evaluations.
- §4 `#ProgramDocs`: the document set and its voting rules. Policy changes normally take effect 90 days after approval (§4.1 `#PolicyDocuments`). The Vendor Questionnaire, the FIAR and the companion mapping table are vendor documents (§4.3 `#VendorDocs`). The lab writes the FIDO Evaluation Report (FER) (§4.4 `#AccreditedLabDocs`).
- §5.1–§5.4 `#L1` `#L1Plus` `#L2` `#L2+`:
  - L1: the Security Secretariat evaluates.
  - L1+: a lab pen-tests, using the software methodology; no companion program.
  - L2: lab documentation review on an AROE.
  - L2+: not yet certifiable.
- §5.5–§5.6 `#L3` `#L3+`: L3 runs through the GP TEE `#L3GP`, CC `#L3CC` or GP FIDO2 SE companion program, with AVA_VAN.3 / AVA_VAN_AP.3. L3+ runs through CC `#L3+CC` or GP FIDO2 SE `#L3+GP`, with AVA_VAN.4. L3 and above are not allowed without a companion program. A Security Target built on the uncertified FIDO2 SE PP is accepted.
- §5.7 `#LevelUpDown`: upgrading means a new certification at the new level, without redoing functional testing. Downgrades go through Secretariat review and keep the original date. Levels never change automatically.
- §6.4–§6.5 `#ApplicationStep` `#SecurityEvalStep`: you apply on the Implementer Dashboard. The boundary is fixed in the VQ, and any change inside it later needs maintenance (§6.5.1 `#VQStep`). The lab reviews the VQ at L2, pen-tests at L1+, and reviews the mapping table and Security Target and then pen-tests at L3/L3+ (§6.5.3 `#L1+Eval`).
- §6.6 `#Issuance`: submit the Certification Request, the approved VQ or mapping table, the FER and fees. FIDO decides within 30 days. A request may be resubmitted three times before it counts as failed. §6.6.2 `#IssuanceStep` explains the certificate number SSSVVVV-SSSSTTTT-LL-PP-DDDDDDDDNNN, which encodes the SR and Test Procedure versions, the level and the program ("FA" for L1–L2).
- §7 `#Maintenance`: the Secretariat reviews the FIAR and classifies the change (§7.1.1 `#FIARReview`):
  - NON-INTERFERING → Derivative: a certificate addendum, no change to FIDO functionality allowed (§7.2 `#Derivative`).
  - MINOR → Delta: only the VQ updates and delta tests are reviewed (§7.3 `#Delta`).
  - MAJOR → Re-certification with reuse of earlier results, for example a new ASP or algorithm, or a change to the boundary architecture (§7.4 `#ReCert`).
  
  Version upgrades and reactivation after suspension also use Delta (§7.5 `#MaintenanceTypes`).
- §8 `#Revocation`: revocation is permanent. Triggers include ignoring a vulnerability, unreported changes, false statements, and more than 180 days in Suspended.
- §9 `#VulnerabilityAssessment`:
  - Triage is RED/AMBER/GREEN/WHITE, each with deadlines (§9.2 `#VulnerabilityTriage`).
  - Attack potential is computed with CEM tables at L1/L2, the L1+ method at L1+, and the JIL/TEE methods at L3/L3+ (§9.3 `#AttackPotentialCalculation`).
  - Per-level tables set the response and corrective-action deadlines (§9.5.2 `#VendorAction`).
  - Missing a deadline leads to suspension, then revocation (§9.5.3 `#DeadlineEnforcement`).
  
  Filing a Delta, revocation request or dispute counts as Corrective Action.
- §10 `#Admin`:
  - Certification states, including Confidential (§10.2 `#CertificationStates`).
  - The Metadata Statement is registered at request time and verified during evaluation; publishing to MDS is optional (§10.3.1 `#Metadata`).
  - TMLA and how products may be described (§10.3.2 `#TMLA`, §10.4 `#Documentation`).
  - Versioning (§10.5 `#ReqVersioning`); dispute resolution (§10.6.1 `#DisputeResolution`).

**What matters most:**
- Certificates have no expiry date in this text. A sunset ends a Test Procedure version's acceptance for new and Delta certifications only. Major changes sunset after 1 year, minor after 6 months, emergencies immediately. Products already approved for evaluation may finish, except in an emergency (§10.5.4.3 `#SunsetDate`).
- The SR v1.5.1 + CTAP 2.1 + GP FIDO2 SE path at L3/L3+ has no sunset date (§10.5.6 `#Exception`).
- Any change inside the boundary needs a FIAR before you can still claim certification (§6.5.1 `#VQStep`, §7.1 `#FIAR`).
- L3 corrective action is mandatory up to Moderate attack potential; L3+ also covers High (§9.5.2.4 `#L3Deadlines`, §9.5.2.5 `#L3+Deadlines`).

**Watch out for:**
- The note in §9.5 `#VulnerabilityResponse` says the response process covers only L1 and L2, yet the section gives tables for L1+, L3 and L3+.
- The policy is inconsistent about which levels use a VQ:
  - §4.3 `#VendorDocs`: L1, L1+, L2 and L2+.
  - §6.1 `#ProcessOverview`: L1 and L2.
  - §6.1.1 `#ProcessSteps`: the VQ for L1+ and the mapping table for L3/L3+. The 1.4.0 history also says the L3 VQ was removed (§1 `#RevHistory`).
  
  SR 1.7 still prints L3 VQ boxes.
- §7.3 `#Delta` puts the Consumer/Enterprise profile change under Delta but calls it "derivative".
- §9.3.1.1 `#L1+Calculation` credits the L1+ table to the smartcard method, although it matches the L1+ document.
- §10.4.3 `#DocumentationEnforcement` points to a nonexistent "Section 9.6.1"; the dispute process is §10.6.1.
