# FIDO Authenticator Security and Privacy Requirements

Version 1.7 · Review Draft · 2026-05-06 · **ACTIVE** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/fido-security-requirements/fido-authenticator-security-requirements-v1.7-rd-20260506.html)

## Summary

The central document of FIDO Authenticator Certification. Each numbered requirement carries its applicable specification (UAF, U2F, FIDO2), profile (Consumer and/or Enterprise), the lowest level it applies to (L1, L1+, L2, L2+, L3, L3+), a calibration box, a mapping box for the L3/L3+ companion programs, the Vendor Questionnaire (VQ) prompt and the test procedure for each level. §3 groups the requirements by topic: key management and RNG, signature counters, passkey backup and syncing, user presence and verification, privacy, physical/side-channel/fault resistance, attestation, operating environment, self-tests and firmware updates, manufacturing, and operational guidance.

§2 explains who evaluates at which level: L1 is a document review by the FIDO Security Secretariat with no lab; from L1+ upward an accredited lab is required, with penetration testing at L1+, L2+, L3 and L3+. It also lists the evidence documents to submit. The Allowed Cryptography List, the AROE List, the Authenticator Metadata Requirements and the Authenticator Certification Policy complete the set.

Status: FIDO lists v1.7 as ACTIVE although the file is labelled Review Draft (2026-05-06). v1.6 remains active until 2027-01-13, and FIDO requires v1.5.1 for L3/L3+.

**Who needs it:** authenticator vendors and certification applicants (all levels), FIDO-accredited security labs

**Look here for:**
- How a requirement entry is laid out (VQ, calibration, companion-program and test-procedure boxes) → §1.4 `#h3_how-to-read-this-document`
- What assurance modes A0–A4 mean for the vendor's evidence and the tester's work → §1.7.1 `#h4_test-assurance-modes`
- What L1, L1+, L2, L2+, L3 and L3+ each mean → §2.1 `#h3_security-levels`
- When a lab is needed, and how the L3/L3+ companion programs and mapping table work → §2.2 `#h3_certification-and-companion-programs`
- Which documents to submit for evaluation → §2.3 `#h3_documents-for-certification`
- What changed between SR versions → Appendix A `#h2_differences-between-versions`

**Changes from previous version:** The 1.6→1.7 appendix entries are titles only, with no body text. They say the UVM extension is clarified as optional, the items a Reset must delete are listed, protocol-feature misuse is prevented, and the VQ boxes for requirements 1.11, 1.12, 3.7 and 5.6 are updated. Confirmed in the body: a new requirement 10.4 (FIDO2, L1 and higher) on FIDO Reset. It requires removing the PIN, biometric data and all credentials, invalidating all PUATs, and restoring defaults such as always-require-UV and minimum PIN length. The other changes were not verified in detail.

## Reading guide

**Start here:**
- §1.4 `#h3_how-to-read-this-document` (non-normative) explains how each requirement entry is built. The header line gives the number, the protocol family (UAF/U2F/FIDO2), the profile (Consumer/Enterprise), the testing style and the lowest level. Optional boxes follow: Note, Relation to Companion Program (L3/L3+ only), Calibration, a Vendor Questionnaire per level, and a Test Procedure per level.
- §1.7.1 `#h4_test-assurance-modes` gives the evidence depth behind each `{A0}`–`{A4}` tag in the Test Procedure boxes.
- §2.2 `#h3_certification-and-companion-programs` says who evaluates at each level and what the companion-program box terms mean.
- §3.1 `#h3_authenticator-definition-derived-requirements`, through requirement 1.1 (the Authenticator boundary). Every later requirement is scoped to that boundary.

**Section by section:**
- §1.3 `#h3_definitions-and-acronyms`: Consumer vs Enterprise Profile (Enterprise Attestation is not allowed in Consumer). It also defines EA mode 1 (vendor-configured RP ID list) and mode 2 (platform-managed), factory reset, and key protection types.
- §1.4–§1.6 `#h3_how-to-read-this-document` `#h3_security-measures`: "L<n> and higher" means level n and everything above it. A few requirements carry level lists ("L1+, L2+ and higher") or apply to L1+ only (5.1.1, 5.8.1, 8.1.1, the L1+ variants of L2 requirements). `(SM-n)` tags point to the Security Reference.
- §1.7 `#h3_testing-style`: testing styles are DaD (documentation exists), GaVR-1/2/3 (GaVR-3 depends on tester skill) and TVFR. The assurance modes:
  - A0: vendor assertion, shown during Functional Certification.
  - A1: the Secretariat checks the rationale.
  - A2: the lab checks the design documentation.
  - A3: adds targeted source/HDL/schematic review. FIPS 140-2 or CC results may be reused.
  - A4: adds operational testing.
  
  In §1.7.2 `#h4_test-procedures---key-words`, "Conduct" pen testing SHALL follow the companion program's style, and the tester MUST keep evidence.
- §2.1 `#h3_security-levels`: at L1+ (§2.1.1 `#h4_enhanced-software-based-authenticator-l1`) the app runs in an untrusted OS and must supply all of its own protection. WBC and obfuscation are not mandatory, but the text says the level is unlikely to be reached without them. Tables 2.1/2.2 in §2.1.2 `#h4_authenticator-hardware-examples` are illustrative only and may not be cited as evidence. They put L2 on an AROE, give L2+ no companion program, map L3 to GP TEE PP or CC SE PPs, and allow only CC at L3+.
- §2.2 `#h3_certification-and-companion-programs`, evaluator by level:
  - L1: Secretariat document review, no lab.
  - L2: lab design-document review only.
  - L1+, L2+, L3 and L3+: lab evaluation with penetration testing.
  
  L3/L3+ applicants MUST answer through a mapping table (SHOULD follow FIDO's). The PPs are Java Card, 3S in SoC (PP-0117), Security IC (PP0084) and GP TEE. NOT COVERED means the Authenticator Application must meet the requirement, state it in the Security Target and have the lab verify it. §2.2.1 `#h4_specific-calibration-for-l1` sets the L1+ evaluation:
  - a white-box pen test of 40 man-days within 10 weeks
  - on the iOS/Android binary, or for SDKs a demo app plus integration guide
  - covering DCA/DFA on WBC, de-obfuscation, and bypass of anti-debug, anti-emulation, anti-root, anti-hook and anti-DBI protections.
- §2.3 `#h3_documents-for-certification`: the evidence set: Security Target, development information, life-cycle, configuration management, guidance, test documentation and the biometric report.
- §3.1 reqs 1.1–1.12 `#h3_authenticator-definition-derived-requirements`:
  - 1.1 boundary: four architecture categories. Categories 1–3 are L1 only, category 2 may reach L1+, category 4 (fully in an AROE) can go higher, and cloud services are never inside the boundary.
  - 1.2 inventory of crypto functions; 1.3 key storage and Credential ID structure.
  - 1.4/1.5 first- vs second-factor (no user entity inside a second-factor raw key handle).
  - Transaction confirmation display: 1.6, 1.9, and 1.11 (L2+).
  - 1.7 RP ID binding via an allowed hash/KDF; 1.10 input validation.
  - 1.12: multi-device credentials MUST support the Device Public Key extension.
- §3.2.1 reqs 2.1.x `#h4_documentation`:
  - ASP inventory, with Secret ASPs flagged; key strength capped by the Allowed Cryptography List (zero for unlisted algorithms).
  - Protection against modification and disclosure (2.1.5/2.1.6, GaVR-3); wrapping of externally stored ASPs (2.1.7–2.1.9).
  - Key handles: authenticity and RP binding (2.1.10/2.1.12); one key per registration (2.1.13).
  - 2.1.14 attestation: "none" cannot be the only type, "self" is excluded from L1+, and multi-device credentials need attestation for the Device Public Key.
  - 2.1.15: Uauth.priv may sign only FIDO assertions (L2+). 2.1.17: factory reset (L2+).
  - 2.1.21: correct BE/BS flags.
- §3.2.2 reqs 2.2.x `#h4_random-number-generation`:
  - An allowed RNG/KDF for keys and for pinToken/pinUvAuthToken.
  - DRNG strength at least the strongest key.
  - Physical TRNG seeding at L3 (2.2.5); the Device Identifier comes from an allowed RNG (2.2.6).
- §3.2.3 `#h4_signature-counters`: counters are optional. The vendor documents per-key vs global counters. Values must strictly increase from >0, or be 0 for "unsupported" or a permanent error.
- §3.2.4 `#h4_backup-and-syncing`: only multi-device credentials and their related ASPs, including sync-fabric attestation keys, may be backup eligible (BEASP). Device Public Keys are excluded. BEASPs count as secret except for backup/restore and MUST be confidential in transport and storage.
- §3.3 reqs 3.x `#h3_authenticator-s-test-for-user-presence-and-user-verification`:
  - 3.1 user presence; 3.2 truthful uv and `userVerificationDetails`.
  - 3.4 cached UV/UP period: the type is fixed at start; time-to-start defaults to 30 s and time-to-complete to 10 min; the period is bound to one RP; cached UP is single-use and ends the period.
  - 3.7 trusted path (L2+). At L2 a dedicated screen counts, and external UV is allowed if declared.
  - 3.8 anti-injection/replay.
  - 3.9 rate limiting. The calibration formula is P(t) ≤ max(170/10000, (24t+16)/10000) at L1, with 12t at L1+/L2. At L2 enforcement must sit inside the AROE, and L3/L3+ use attack potential.
  - 3.10 biometrics at L2+ need BioLevel 1+/2+; 3.11 GetInfo must agree with UV claims.
  - 3.3, 3.5 and 3.6 were removed.
- §3.4 reqs 4.x `#h3_privacy`:
  - No cross-RP correlation handle; shared attestation keys need batches of ≥100,000 (Consumer).
  - Unlinkable credential IDs; UAF deregister and username rules.
  - 4.6/4.7: credProtect is mandatory, with level-dependent non-disclosure that covers on-device displays too.
  - 4.10–4.12 Enterprise Attestation: Consumer MUST NOT support it (firmware EA must be disabled so only the vendor can enable it); the mode-1 RP ID list can be changed only by the vendor.
- §3.5 reqs 5.x `#h3_physical-security-side-channel-attack-resistance-and-fault-injection-resistance`:
  - Documentation (5.1 at L2, 5.1.1 for L1+); tamper resistance (5.3, L3).
  - Key-use limits (5.4, from L1+); leakage (5.5, L3).
  - Remote timing (5.6); constant time (5.7, L3).
  - Debug interfaces disabled (5.8) or debugging detected (5.8.1, L1+).
  - Fault resistance (5.9, at L1+ and at L3 and higher).
  - 5.10: no vendor features hidden in exclude/allow lists (L1).
- §3.6 reqs 6.x `#h3_attestation`: attestation certificate per model (L1+), metadata accuracy (6.3, see Metadata Requirements), model extension when the root is shared (6.4), one key per EA identifier (6.6).
- §3.7 reqs 7.x `#h3_operating-environment`:
  - L2+ needs an AROE from the AROE List (7.1), with OS security functions active, isolation, no OS modification, and security configuration controlled by the vendor.
  - 7.7: the vendor must check that the customer's EA RP IDs belong to the customer.
- §3.8 reqs 8.x `#h3_self-tests-and-firmware-updates`:
  - Self-test alternatives (8.1/8.1.1/8.3): fault resistance per 5.9, known-answer tests (KAT) or pairwise checks, or signed-firmware verification.
  - 8.2 signed updates (L1); 8.4 verify each signature before output if not fault resistant.
  - New 8.5: any binary change MUST bump the reported firmware version (GetInfo, HID INIT, UAF AuthenticatorVersion).
- §3.9 reqs 9.x `#h3_manufacturing-and-development`: allowed RNG for factory keys, access control on attestation keys and provisioning equipment, revision control with unique configuration-item IDs, control of factory personnel.
- §3.10 `#h3_operational-guidance`:
  - 10.1: EA products are sold only to enterprises.
  - 10.3: no misuse of protocol fields (allow-list tunnelling, extra data in Credential IDs).
  - New 10.4 FIDO Reset (FIDO2, L1+): MUST remove PIN, biometrics, credentials, largeBlob data and BLE pairings. It also regenerates the Device ID, renews the Credential Store State, disables EA, invalidates all PUATs including persistent ones, and resets alwaysUv, minimum PIN length and long-touch-for-reset.
- Appendix A `#h2_differences-between-versions` is the change history. Appendix B `#h2_old-version-of-table-2.2` (non-normative) explains why JIL, TEE and L1+ ratings are not comparable.

**What matters most:**
- The boundary (1.1) and the ASP inventory (2.1.1/2.1.2) frame the whole evaluation. Keep them consistent across the VQ, Security Target and metadata. `#h3_authenticator-definition-derived-requirements` `#h4_documentation`
- Read the level tag as a floor. For L1+, look for the L1+-only variants (5.1.1, 5.8.1, 8.1.1) and the L1+ calibration text. `#h3_how-to-read-this-document` `#h4_specific-calibration-for-l1`
- Requirement 3.4: its note says FIDO2 implementations must use the 30 s / 10 min base values, bind each period to one RP and treat cached UP as single-use. This is easy to break in pinUvAuthToken handling. `#h3_authenticator-s-test-for-user-presence-and-user-verification`
- 3.9 retry limits must satisfy the formula for the target level. Labs check the arithmetic for PIN and biometric FAR. `#h3_authenticator-s-test-for-user-presence-and-user-verification`
- credProtect (4.6/4.7) and the attestation-type rules (2.1.14) apply to FIDO2 from L1. `#h3_privacy` `#h4_documentation`
- Check the 10.4 reset list item by item against your authenticatorReset code. It goes beyond 2.1.17's factory-reset rule. `#h3_operational-guidance`
- At L3/L3+, every NOT COVERED or PARTIALLY COVERED item must be met by the application and appear in your Security Target. `#h3_certification-and-companion-programs`
- **Preparing a submission:**
  - Pick the level.
  - Collect the §2.3 documents.
  - Answer the VQ box for your level in each applicable requirement (or explain why it doesn't apply); at L3/L3+, the mapping table.
  - Check the Allowed Crypto and AROE lists.
  - For L1+, line up a lab for the white-box pen test. `#h3_documents-for-certification`

**Watch out for:**
- The file is labelled Review Draft, yet the catalog lists it as ACTIVE.
- §1.4 says VQ boxes are for L1, L1+ and L2 only. §2.2 says a questionnaire is needed at every level, and nearly every requirement still carries L3 GP/L3/L3+ VQ boxes. No requirement has L2+ boxes.
- The note in §3.1 says 2.1.15 is "L2+ and higher", but the requirement header says L2 and higher.
- In 3.8 the UVM-extension indication is now "if supported", while the L2 calibration of 3.7 still has no such qualifier.
- Appendix A lists VQ updates for 1.11, 1.12, 3.7 and 5.6, and its 1.6→1.7 entries have no body text. In my comparison of §3 against v1.6, the box changes I found were a new L2 VQ/TP pair on 2.1.15, the new 8.5, 10.3 and 10.4, and the 3.8 wording. I did not see the claimed VQ edits.
- 5.10 and 10.3 overlap. Number 10.2 is unused, and 3.3, 3.5, 3.6 and 5.2 are removed placeholders.
