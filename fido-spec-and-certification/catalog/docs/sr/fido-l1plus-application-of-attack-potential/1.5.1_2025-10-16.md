# Application of attack potential to FIDO L1+ Authenticator

Version 1.5.1 · Final Document · 2025-10-16 · **latest published** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/fido-security-requirements/FIDO-L1%2B-Application-of-Attack-Potential-v1.5.1-fd-20251016.html)

## Summary

The rating method labs use when evaluating L1+ authenticators, which are software authenticators protected by software techniques such as code obfuscation and white-box cryptography. It adapts ISO/IEC 18045 Annex B, the smartcard attack-potential method and Annex A of the TEE PP to software. Factors are elapsed time, expertise, knowledge of the TOE, window of opportunity, equipment and replicability, each scored separately for the identification and exploitation phases. Only complete attack paths are rated; partial attacks are steps within them. A summed score of 26 or more (Moderate) meets L1+, and 12 or more (Basic) meets L1. §7 gives examples of partial attacks on code and white-box crypto protections, and full attacks by local and remote expert attackers. Worked rating examples are in a separate attack-methods document. SR §2.2 points here, since L1+ has no companion program.
