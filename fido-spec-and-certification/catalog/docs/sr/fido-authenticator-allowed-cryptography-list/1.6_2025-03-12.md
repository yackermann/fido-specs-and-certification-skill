# FIDO Authenticator Allowed Cryptography List

Version 1.6 · Final Document · 2025-03-12 · **ACTIVE until 2027-01-13** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/fido-security-requirements/fido-authenticator-allowed-cryptography-list-v1.6-fd-20250312.html)

## Summary

v1.6 (Final, 2025-03-12) of the list of algorithms allowed inside certified authenticators, each with the security strength used for the Overall Claimed Security Strength. Its algorithm content is the same as v1.7. Status: ACTIVE until its sunset on 2027-01-13.

**Who needs it:** authenticator vendors, FIDO-accredited security labs

**Look here for:**
- Allowed signature algorithms and strengths → §3.9 `#h3_signature-algorithms`
- Key agreement, including ML-KEM → §3.7 `#h3_key-agreement-algorithms`
- RNG/DRBG requirements → §3.6 `#h3_random-number-generator`
- Post-quantum background → §3.1 `#h3_post-quantum-cryptography`

**Changes from previous version:** Compared with the v1.5.1 text, which follows the older baseline even though it was published later: v1.6 adds ML-KEM (FIPS 203) for key agreement and ML-DSA (FIPS 204) for signatures. It replaces the outdated NIST round-3 PQC narrative with a NIST quantum security category table. References move to FIPS 186-5, SP 800-108r1-upd1 and SP 800-56C r2. RNGs validated under either FIPS 140-2 or FIPS 140-3 are accepted. It adds a note that SHA3 was removed from CNSA 2.0, and the SOGIS PKCS#1 v1.5 legacy date moves to 2030.

## Reading guide

**Use this version when:** you certify against the v1.6 Security Requirements set before its sunset on 2027-01-13, or you want a Final (not Review Draft) text with the current algorithm tables. Labs and vendors can treat it as the stable form of what v1.7 says.

**Differences from v1.7:** I compared the two texts line by line, and the normative content is the same. Every algorithm table, footnote and note in §2 `#h2_requirements-for-additional-candidates` and §3.2–§3.10 is unchanged. The only differences are these:
- §3.1 `#h3_post-quantum-cryptography`: says one of BIKE or HQC could be standardised. v1.7 says HQC is next.
- The §2 note cites NIST IR 8547 with a broken reference tag, and the reference list has no IR 8547 entry. v1.7 fixes both.
- Status text: Final Requirements Document here, Review Draft in v1.7.

**Differences from v1.5.1** (which is older in content even though its date is later):
- §3.7 `#h3_key-agreement-algorithms` adds ML-KEM-512/768/1024, and §3.9 `#h3_signature-algorithms` adds ML-DSA-44/65/87.
- §3.1 replaces the round-3 candidate narrative with the NIST category table and FIPS 203/204. A note in §2 warns the 112-bit floor may rise.
- §2 is reworded around adding a function to the list, rather than using one internally or claiming non-zero strength. ANSSI and BSI are added as example endorsers.
- §3.6 `#h3_random-number-generator` accepts FIPS 140-2 or 140-3 validations, and the TRNG path is SP 800-90B rather than the SP 800-90C NRBG table. The RNG subsections lose their numbers and their anchors change (`#physical-true-trng-non-deterministic-random-number-bit-generator-nrbg-requirements`, `#deterministic-random-number-drng-bit-generator-drbg-requirements`).
- §3.10 `#h3_anonymous-attestation-algorithms` drops the paragraph that mapped embedding-field sizes to 112/128 bits.
- References move to FIPS 186-5, SP 800-108r1-upd1 and SP 800-56C r2. Also added: the CNSA 2.0 SHA3 note, the FIPS 140 curve-restriction note in §3.9, and SOGIS PKCS#1 v1.5 legacy use until 2030 (previously 2022).

**Section by section:** structure as in v1.7; see that page's reading guide.
