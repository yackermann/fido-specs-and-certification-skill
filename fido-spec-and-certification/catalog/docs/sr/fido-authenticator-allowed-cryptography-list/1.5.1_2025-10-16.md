# FIDO Authenticator Allowed Cryptography List

Version 1.5.1 · Final Document · 2025-10-16 · **ACTIVE, no expiry (L3/L3+)** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/fido-security-requirements/fido-authenticator-allowed-cryptography-list-v1.5.1-fd-20251016.html)

## Summary

v1.5.1 (Final, 2025-10-16) of the Allowed Cryptography List. It belongs to the v1.5.1 CTAP 2.1 / L3/L3+ track and keeps the pre-1.6 algorithm content. It has no ML-KEM or ML-DSA entries, still cites FIPS 186-4 and SP 800-108, and describes PQC as not yet standardised. RNGs validated under Common Criteria or FIPS 140-3 are accepted. §2 frames new-algorithm requests in terms of internal use or claiming a non-zero strength. Status: ACTIVE with no expiry, together with SR v1.5.1 for L3/L3+.

**Who needs it:** vendors certifying CTAP 2.1 authenticators against SR v1.5.1 (L3/L3+), FIDO-accredited security labs

- Allowed algorithms and claimed strengths → §3 `#h2_allowed-cryptographic-functions`

- RNG requirements → §3.6 `#h3_random-number-generator`
