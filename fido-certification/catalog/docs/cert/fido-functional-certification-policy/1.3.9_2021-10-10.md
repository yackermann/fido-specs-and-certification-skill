# Functional Certification

Version 1.3.9 · Final · 2021-10-10 · **ACTIVE** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Functional_Certification_Program_Policy_v1.3.9_FINAL.pdf)

## Summary

The Functional Certification Program Policy governs FIDO functional (conformance and interoperability) certification for U2F, UAF and FIDO2 servers, clients and authenticators. Each implementation is certified on its own. Certification passes through three steps: Conformance Self-Validation with the FIDO test tools, interoperability testing at an Interoperability Event or through On Demand testing, and Certification Issuance. Applicants submit a Certification Request, test tool results, interop results, a signed Vendor Self-Assertion Checklist and fees. UAF and U2F authenticators must also register metadata. Authenticators do not receive a Functional Certificate. They must pass Level 1 Authenticator Certification, with some L1 security requirements demonstrated during interop, and then follow the Authenticator Certification Policy. The policy also covers the First Implementer and Universal Server programs, derivative certification, use of the certification mark, revocation and disputes.

Two sunset rules apply. A superseded specification version is retired at least 6 months (servers) or 18 months (clients and authenticators) after the new version's Certification Availability Date. Policy versions follow Major.Minor.Patch: a Minor version sunsets 90 days after publication, and a Patch version replaces its predecessor immediately. In v1.3.9, CTAP1/CTAP2 certification is clarified and the authenticator profile (consumer, enterprise, hybrid) appears on certificates. The document is PDF only, so the references below give section numbers and pages instead of anchors.

**Who needs it:** certification applicants (server, client and authenticator vendors), relying parties deploying derivative servers
