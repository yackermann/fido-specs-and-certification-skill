# FIDO Authenticator Certification Policy

Version 1.4.1 · Final Document · 2025-10-14 · **latest published (the certification-levels page still links v1.3 PDF)** · [source](https://fidoalliance.org/specs/certification/authenticator/authenticator-policy-v1.4.1-fd-20251014.html)

## Summary

The rules of the FIDO Authenticator Certification Program. It covers roles and program documents; the levels L1, L1+, L2, L2+, L3 and L3+, with the L3/L3+ companion programs (GlobalPlatform TEE, Common Criteria and GlobalPlatform FIDO2 SE); and the step-by-step process. That process runs from preparation, through Functional Certification as a prerequisite and the application, to the security evaluation: Vendor Questionnaire, Secretariat review at L1, lab evaluation at L1+ and above, then report review and issuance. It also covers maintenance through the FIDO Impact Analysis Report (derivative, delta, re-certification), suspension and revocation, and vulnerability handling with per-level attack-potential calculations and vendor deadlines. Under §10.5, a new version of the security test procedures becomes active on its availability date and the previous one sunsets after 1 year for a major change, 6 months for a minor one, or immediately in an emergency.

Its revision history lists one change in v1.4.1 (2025-10-14) from v1.4.0: the §10.5.6 exception. CTAP 2.1 products certified against SR v1.5.1 at L3/L3+ may use the GlobalPlatform FIDO2 SE companion program with no sunset date. Status: latest published version. FIDO's certification-levels page still links the v1.3 PDF.

**Who needs it:** certification applicants and authenticator vendors, FIDO-accredited security labs
